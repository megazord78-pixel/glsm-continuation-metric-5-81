Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript “An Interval-Certified Positivity Theorem for
a Corrected GLSM Metric along a Calabi--Yau Continuation Path.”

The paper gives a computer-assisted theorem on the explicit compact toric
Calabi--Yau threefold with Hodge numbers `(5,81)` used in a type-IIB
compactification model.  An 83-real parameterized Krawczyk construction
certifies one locally unique implicit branch on `d in [1.49985,1.5]`.  Along
that complete interval, 1003 adjacent interval segments prove positive
definiteness of a frozen corrected `3 x 3` Hermitian GLSM pullback metric.
The proof includes a second formula implementation on the weakest segment of
each metric patch and an independent endpoint root and determinant replay.

The contribution is methodological and deliberately narrow.  The manuscript
does not claim a global Ricci-flat metric, a derivation of the physical
alpha-prime correction, or a complete string vacuum.  The correction is an
exactly frozen gauge-invariant `ddbar` ansatz; the theorem concerns its
positive definiteness along the stated path.

The complete proof record, BSD-licensed checker, mutation tests, and
transitive SHA-256 provenance graph accompany the submission.  The final
checker uses only the Python standard library, allowing editors and referees
to replay the exact rational inequalities with one command.

This manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has
one author.  No conflict of interest is declared.  No external funding was
used for this work.
