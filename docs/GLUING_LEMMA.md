# Branch-gluing lemma

Let `P_i` be the 1,084 closed parameter boxes and `X_i` their certified
83-real-dimensional uniqueness boxes. Each parametric Krawczyk inclusion gives
a locally unique regular root map `x_i : P_i -> X_i`.

At the centre of `P_i` with transverse coordinate `u=0`, the previously
certified robust continuation root lies in a strict sub-box of `X_i`. Hence the
two roots agree there. On the connected central slice of `P_i`, the equality
set is nonempty, open by local uniqueness and closed by continuity. Therefore
`x_i` agrees with the robust continuation branch on the entire central slice.

Every consecutive pair `P_i,P_(i+1)` has a connected nonempty product overlap.
The two roots agree at `u=0` in this overlap. Applying the same open--closed
argument on the connected overlap proves `x_i=x_(i+1)` there. Induction glues
all local roots into one parameterized branch over the full product tube.

The quick verifier checks every finite hypothesis of this lemma: exact parent
inclusions, strict nesting of robust anchors, the 1,083 positive longitudinal
overlap widths, and the exact transverse product tiling.
